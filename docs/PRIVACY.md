# Rolodex Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Rolodex is a Mac app for browsing, editing and organizing the contacts in the macOS Contacts app.

## What Rolodex collects

Nothing. Rolodex does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data.

- **No accounts.** Rolodex has no sign-in and no user accounts.
- **No analytics or tracking.** Rolodex contains no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting frameworks.
- **No third parties.** Because no data is collected, none is shared with anyone.

## Your contacts

Rolodex reads and edits your contacts through the macOS Contacts framework, with the permission you grant in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Contacts. Your contacts stay in Contacts. If you sync Contacts with iCloud or another account, your changes sync the same way they would from the Contacts app. Rolodex never sends your contacts anywhere else.

## Maps

When you open a contact's address on the map, Rolodex uses Apple Maps (MapKit) to show and locate that address. The address is sent to Apple for this purpose only, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Nothing else leaves your Mac.

## What Rolodex stores on your Mac

Smart group rules, hidden groups, blocked contacts, and a short history of your edits are saved in Rolodex's own folder on your Mac. They are removed when you delete the app's data.

## Children

Rolodex does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
